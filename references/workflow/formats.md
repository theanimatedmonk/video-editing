# Formats: the router's menu

When asked to "edit this", pick ONE format from what the inputs and request are, name it in the asset plan, and follow its recipe. If two fit, ask.

| Format | Pick when | Shape | Builds on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Talking-head reel** | one person speaking to camera, vertical, under 90 s | 9:16, dead air cut, punch-ins every other cut, 2–3 word captions, hook in first 2 s | `rough-cut.md`, HyperFrames `/embedded-captions` |
| **Talking head + overlays** | interview, podcast or explainer with points worth showing | footage untouched, kinetic titles, lower thirds, data callouts synced to words | HyperFrames `/talking-head-recut` |
| **Podcast clips** | long conversation, wants several shorts | find 3–5 self-contained 30–60 s moments (hook line + payoff), cut each, captions, speaker labels | `watch-reference.md` to find moments, then talking-head reel |
| **Product promo** | app recordings, product photos, a launch | 20–40 s, hook → proof in the real UI → turn → CTA, carried seams, one ground colour | `../glide-cut.md`, `../number-to-orbit.md`, HyperFrames moment-film |
| **Screen-recording explainer** | a demo or tutorial recording + voice | zoom to each click, cursor emphasis, step titles, cut waiting time | `rough-cut.md` on the voice, HyperFrames keyframes for zooms |
| **Faceless explainer** | a topic or article, no footage | voiceover + designed scenes, one idea per scene | HyperFrames `/faceless-explainer` |
| **Beat-synced montage** | music track + many clips or photos | cuts on the beat grid, energy follows the track | HyperFrames `/music-to-video` |
| **Style match** | "make it like this video" | measured from the reference | `style-from-reference.md`, then `references/styles/<name>.md` |

Add a row whenever a new style is learned.
