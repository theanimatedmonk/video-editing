# Style notes (calls that landed)

- **Type:** bold system sans for headlines (`ui-sans-serif, system-ui`, weight 800, letter-spacing −0.035em). A refined serif (Instrument Serif) was tried and rejected as less punchy.
- **Transitions:** smooth and cool beats flashy. Glide cuts with a horizontal blur at peak speed; one object carrying across each cut.
- **Text:** soft arrival + steady glide (no slam-and-creep), glitch exit when leaving.
- **Photos as cards:** small cards can scale up to fill the frame one after another (expo.inOut ~1.2 s, corner radius shrinking as they grow), then the next card lands on top and does the same.
- **Characters:** detailed, shaded vector birds that bank along arcs read as premium; flat blobs on a spinning circle read as cheap.
- **Ground:** one dark ground colour (#07080D) for the whole film; colour comes from cards, accents (#F5C26B amber, #6FE3D0 teal) and an aurora window on the end card.
